About

Small studio, big ideas.

Lumio Games is an independent studio from Wageningen in the Netherlands. We build everything ourselves: game code, physics, worlds, cars and characters.

Our games are easy to start and hard to put down. They are made for short sessions with friends, on the couch or online.
