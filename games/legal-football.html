The game

Pick a team. Win the World Cup. Probably.

Play a quick exhibition match, fight your way through a full league season, or survive the group stage and knockouts of the World Cup, penalty shoot-outs included.

Prefer real opponents? Host or join a match online, or just invite a friend through Steam. Men's and women's teams are both in.

Features

☂

Wild weather

Sunshine, rain, snow, fog, and wind that grows into a full storm. Day or night under the floodlights.

🎙

Live commentary

Two commentary pairs, British or American, react to every goal, miss and bad tackle.

👕

Make your kit

Stripes, hoops, blocks or rings, in any colour. Choose your formation and how many players a side.

🟨

Fouls & drama

Yellow cards, red cards, injuries and substitutions. Playing on a man down is part of the fun.

⏪

Replays

Watch your best goals again in slow motion from different cameras, and save them as video.

📣

Full stadiums

Pick the stadium and the crowd size. Win and celebrate with sparks or confetti.
