The game

Synthwave dreams at 300 km/h.

Neon Velocity is a love letter to 80s arcade racers. Rain hits the road, the city lights bounce off the asphalt and the synth soundtrack kicks in.

The handling is easy to learn: grip in the corners, pull the handbrake to drift, shift through six gears. Lose a race? Rewind the tape VHS-style and try again.
